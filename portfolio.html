GreatKey Educational Consult

Empowering learners through a comprehensive digital platform for tuition and homeschooling.

The Challenge

GreatKey Educational Consult offers a wide range of services, from home tuition to adult education. However, their previous online presence didn't effectively communicate the breadth of their offerings, making it difficult for parents and students to find the specific programs they needed.

  • Unclear service categorization
  • Difficulty in booking home tuition
  • Limited visibility for vacation classes
  • Lack of digital resources for homeschooling

Our Solution

We developed a structured, informative website that clearly segments their diverse services. Features include a dedicated section for "Home Tuition" with tutor profiles, an easy inquiry form for vacation classes, and a resource hub for homeschooling parents.

  • Clear, segmented service pages
  • Simple inquiry and registration forms
  • Mobile-friendly resource access
  • SEO optimized for educational keywords

The Results

200% Enrollment Increase
50+ New Tutors
98% Parent Satisfaction
National Service Reach

GreatKey Educational Consult has seen a surge in inquiries and enrollments. The new platform has streamlined their administrative process, allowing them to focus on what matters most: delivering quality education.

Corporate / Marketing

GripNGo

Creating a modern, premium marketing website to capture leads and drive conversions.

The Challenge

GripNGo needed a high-end marketing presence to outshine competitors. Their previous digital approach was outdated and lacked the interactivity required to engage modern users. They needed a clean, playful, yet premium aesthetic to effectively showcase their value proposition.

  • Low conversion rates on legacy landing pages
  • Outdated, non-premium aesthetic
  • Lack of user engagement and interactivity
  • Poor mobile experience

Our Solution

We designed a completely new marketing website utilizing a sleek dark-mode aesthetic with vibrant accents. We implemented smooth scrolling, micro-animations, and dynamic case studies that guide the user journey naturally toward conversion.

  • Premium custom UI with glassmorphism
  • Engaging micro-animations and scroll reveals
  • Optimized user flow for lead generation
  • Fully responsive design with accessibility features

The Results

40% Conversion Increase
5x More Leads
1.5s Load Time
100/100 Performance Score

GripNGo's new digital presence instantly positioned them as a premium offering in their market. The improved user experience and performance optimizations resulted in a dramatic increase in qualified leads within the first month of launch.
